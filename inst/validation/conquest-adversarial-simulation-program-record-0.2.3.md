## Priority reassessment

Candidate 004 remains an immutable, bounded fixed-artifact record without an
active promotion target. Its independent-review handoff is neither deleted nor
reported complete, but it no longer blocks a separate population-level
simulation program. An external reviewer may later challenge the design or
audit final evidence; reviewer identity is not a scientific substitute for
truth-known generation, independent mathematical oracles, complete
denominators, and adversarial controls.

The new selected question is:

> Within a prospectively frozen RSM/PCM design population, where are mfrmr and
> ConQuest each correct against generating truth and independent oracles, and

thresholds, and its numerical pass cannot be reused to accept the new claim.

## Failure-mode coverage

Nine scenario classes are fixed: a disjoint complete-crossing reference,
connected sparse multiple-bridge transport, weak single-bridge sensitivity,
unequal Rater workload, paired planned/explicit missingness, rare boundary
categories, extreme Person scores, an unused-intermediate-category negative
control, and a disconnected-design negative control. Both RSM and PCM belong
to the intended envelope, but six template extensions remain required before
any generated response is permitted.

The scenario-by-family dataset—not a fit, parameter, row, or quadrature node—is
the independent sampling unit. Every generated dataset must stay in the
unconditional denominator. Conditional numerical summaries require their
unconditional completion, eligibility, and failure companion counts.

## Evidence layers

The program separates:

- structural eligibility and expected rejection;
- unconditional ConQuest and mfrmr execution outcomes;
- joint numerical eligibility;
- probability truth error and continuous-target oracle error;
- parameter bias and RMSE;
- common-coordinate cross-engine differences;
- quadrature sensitivity; and
- false-ready or false-pass decisions.

Uncertainty coverage is explicitly deferred until a common covariance
estimand, interval construction, and boundary rule are proven. A missing or
failed fit cannot disappear from the program merely because a conditional
numeric metric is unavailable.

## Calibration and confirmation

Smoke, calibration, and confirmation datasets must use disjoint seed bands.
Smoke data establish mechanics only. Calibration estimates computational cost,
failure frequency, and metric variability but cannot enter the confirmation
analysis. Confirmation cannot begin until metric-specific decision loss,
precision or MCSE targets, replication counts, and stop/expand/abort rules are
frozen. No universal replication count is implied.

## Unclosed design fields

Seven fields intentionally block generation:

1. exact DGP parameter values;
2. a neutral generator and independent-oracle implementation;
3. the non-evaluative smoke seed band;
4. the calibration seed band;
5. the untouched confirmation seed band;
6. metric-specific precision targets and replication counts; and
7. sequential stop/expand/abort rules plus the runtime/storage cap.
